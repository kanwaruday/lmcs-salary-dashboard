Office Order
Ref.: HES/MD(Op.)/UK/OO/06/21 Date: 8th February 2021
Subject: Daily Reporting by Fee Clerks — WhatsApp Format

Fee Clerk Daily Reporting

To prevent miscommunication and ensure accountability, Fee Clerks at all schools are required to submit a daily report by 6:00 PM every evening via WhatsApp.

Daily Report Format

Field What to Report
A. Hourly Work Done Report everything done during each hour of the working day
B. No. of Parents Attended Number of distinct parents attended during the day
C. Parent Feedback Summary of major things said by parents (positive and negative) — as PRO incharge
D. Employee Leave Status Names of employees under each leave category (present, absent, on leave, etc.)
E. Outside School Document Received Record of letters / correspondences received from external people, organisations, or authorities
F. Cash in Hand Petty Cash: Amount held for day-to-day school expenses
School Fee Cash: Fee cash not yet deposited on that day
Other Cash: Cash from other sources (VVM, Scholastic, Annual Fee, etc.)

Substitution by IT Teacher

In case a Fee Clerk is on leave, the IT Teacher (Computer Teacher) shall be responsible for fees and other minor duties of the Fee Clerk. Even then, the Fee Clerk must fill in fields D, E, and F remotely.
