Subject: Student Incident Report — Procedure & Guidelines

Incident Report Form

The Student Incident Report is a structured document used to record and address various incidents involving students within a school environment. It ensures all relevant details are captured systematically, promoting transparency, accountability, and appropriate resolution.

Why File an Incident Report?

  • Parent may get involved: Situations requiring parental involvement require thorough documentation.
  • Parent may question later: Parents may enquire about incidents after the fact.
  • Outside school individuals are involved: When non-school individuals are part of the incident.
  • Escalation beyond the Principal: Serious cases requiring referral to higher authorities.

When to File an Incident Report

  • Student injuries / medical emergency
  • Student fights / violence
  • External complaints
  • Feedback from parents
  • Major damage at school
  • Substance abuse
  • Theft cases
  • Behavioural issues
  • Misuse of technology
  • Chronic absenteeism (more than 1 week)
  • Academic deviation
  • Sexual assault
  • Corporal punishment

How to Fill the Incident Report Form

1. Student Information

  • Collect and record the student's full name
  • Note the student's class

2. Incident Details

  • Record the date and time when the incident occurred
  • Specify the location (e.g., class period, subject)
  • Provide a detailed description of the incident, including sequence of events and direct quotes if relevant

3. Summaries by Individuals Involved & Witnesses

  • Statement by the student / group of students involved (one side of the story)
  • Statement by the student / group of students involved (other side of the story)
  • Statement by a neutral student witness (preferably a school appointee)
  • Statement by the teacher who witnessed the incident
  • Statement by the helping staff who witnessed the incident

4. Student Background

Ask the student the following questions to gauge background context:

  • Parents' / guardians' occupations and time of reaching home
  • Student's daily routine
  • Names of 2 best friends at school
  • What the student does after school
  • Form an opinion about their background based on responses

5. Academic & Behavioural Feedback

  • Collect feedback from all subject teachers
  • Class teacher must include the following scores:
    • Class Discipline (CD)
    • Class Response (CR)
    • Homework (HW)
    • Class Work (CW)
    • CWA average score of the ongoing semester

6. Action Taken

  • Document immediate actions taken at the time of the incident (medical attention, separation of students, notification of authorities, etc.)

7. Follow-Up Actions

  • Actions taken by the teacher after the incident
  • Actions taken by the student after the incident
  • Actions taken by the parents after the incident
  • Any other relevant remarks or observations

8. Principal Sign-Off

  • The Principal must sign and date the document to confirm review and completion
