Interview Talking Points

Screening & outreach — reference for the Principal Interview

🏫 Hiring Dashboard
Work through these topics during the Principal Interview stage. This is a reference script, not a form — nothing here is saved. Once the interview is done, record the actual ratings and notes via the Interview Report (linked from the Hiring Dashboard).
Source: LMCS HR — "Questionnaire for Teachers during Interview"
1
Spouse's Occupation
  • Ask about the spouse's jobUnderstand scope of transfer and monetary needs
2
Family Setup
  • Nuclear vs. joint familyFor understanding available support
  • Ask about kids — age, school
3
Residence
  • Ease of reaching school from home
4
Academic Qualification
  • Evaluate the type of school attended
  • Subject background and combination clarity (SSc)
  • Marks in Class 10 and 12
  • Private vs. Regular — flag if Bachelor's is Division III
  • Open schooling?
  • Any gap between years — ask why
  • CTET / TET / D.El.Ed / B.Ed status
  • Professional qualifications
5
Computer Literacy
  • Word, Excel, PowerPoint, general proficiency
6
Experience
  • School or elsewhere?
  • How long? Periods taken? Free periods? School timing? Classes taken? Subjects taken? Salary taken?
7
Extra Hobbies
  • Dance / music — relevant for the Annual Function
8
Commitment to Serve
  • Ask about their reasons for applying
  • Married / unmarried / family way
9
Minimum Monthly Salary
  • Ask their floor — below which we shouldn't call them back
  • If they quote high: evaluate the whole form and question further (language, divisions, subject combos, non-core subjects)
10
Overall Things to Look At
  • Confidence
  • English
  • Strengths & weaknesses
  • Demo class, if required
  • Student feedback
